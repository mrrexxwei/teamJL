About Us

We are Taconic's horticulture branch, teaching students plant science while offering plants for sale to the school and community. From landscaping projects to decorative plant arrangements, we ensure every plant is cared for with knowledge and dedication.

Our programs emphasize hands-on learning, sustainable practices, and community engagement. Students gain practical experience in propagation, greenhouse management, and retail operations.
